\section{Concordance with the source document}
\label{app:concordance}

The re-architecture in this report is a change of \emph{order}, not of content:
the source's node-by-node parts were re-sequenced into a physics arc
(\hyperref[sec:edition]{A note on this edition}), which dissolves the S11--S16
labels, moves the weak sector in front of the iron core, joins MESA's network
solver and its return contract to the computational setting, and places the two
audit passes before the register they feed. That makes a concordance necessary
rather than decorative: a source part's argument can now land in two places, and
this appendix is where you find them.

Everything below is mechanically checked by
\code{study/tier3-report/check\_coverage.py}.

\begin{aside}
Nineteen source headings are unnumbered in the original (the title, the three
front-matter blocks, the eleven part titles, the two register sub-headings, the
closing handoff block and the References); they carry ``---'' in the first
column. The source's irregular identifiers --- \S I.2b, \S I.6b, \S II.1b,
\S III.4b, \S IV.8b, \S V.5b and \S V.5c --- are reproduced as they stand.
The four re-homed blocks are the ones set in bold: source \S0.7 (three
subsections), \S I.2 and \S I.2b, Parts IX and X, and the Part-0 self-check
whose questions are redistributed (\S\ref{app:selfcheckmoves}).
\end{aside}

\begin{aside}
\textbf{The source's own cross-reference conventions.} The source states that
within it ``Part~0 sections are \S0.x and node sections are \S I.x (S11),
\S II.x (S12), \ldots''; every such reference in this report has been replaced
by a live \S-link to the section listed in the table above, and the two
sentences whose target changed direction under the re-ordering (the opener of
\S\ref{part:weak} and ``the runaway of \S0.5.2'' in \S\ref{sec:ecratchet}; the
register pointer in \S\ref{sec:converging}) are reworded to say so, each marked
with a TeX comment in the source file. No other wording was changed.
\end{aside}
