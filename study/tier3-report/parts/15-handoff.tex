\subsection{What Phase 0 established}
\label{sec:established}

Positively, and in order of how load-bearing each is:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Conservation by construction works and costs nothing.}
  $\dd Y = \nu\Phi$ through a fixed integer matrix gives exactly-zero column
  drifts and random-flux drift within $\max(10^{-12}s,\,10^{-13}G)$ over random
  fluxes spanning twelve decades within each draw, at overall $\varphi$ scales
  $10^{0}\ldots10^{-20}$, with $\dd\Ye$ surviving through the weak columns. The
  gate blocks training and has never failed since the export was fixed.
  \resultsref{2026-07-08/09}
\item \textbf{The rate pipeline is trustworthy to 0.004 dex} against an
  independent Fortran code, with every one of 135 severe outliers
  class-explained or open-flagged. \resultsref{2026-07-10}
\item \textbf{The reference integrator produces valid $\Phi$ labels} with
  machine-precision internal identities and an energy residual three orders
  inside the 1\% gate --- for $\Tn < 5$, at 4--40 core-days for a useful
  subset. \resultsref{2026-07-12}
\item \textbf{The bridge structure is real and concentrated.} Top-20
  inter-group columns carry 0.88 of the flux (\msn{80}); the literature
  bottleneck \nuc{Sc}{45}(p,$\gamma$)\nuc{Ti}{46}
  \citep{woosley1973, hix1996} is recovered independently at rank 2/251
  (\msn{151}). \resultsref{2026-07-12} That the quasi-equilibrium picture of
  \citet{bodansky1968} survives on relaxed states is the author's gloss on
  those rows, not a RESULTS statement.
\item \textbf{Target A is viable and is the decision}, unconditionally below
  4~GK.
\end{enumerate}

  (\S\ref{sec:epistemics}). Train on labels that reproduce a bug, or on

  validity domain (\reg{T-03}).

  (\S\ref{sec:epistemics}). The published baseline was trained on the same

  dry-run-clean patch that is deliberately not applied, because applying it
  would change what ``the label configuration'' means.
\item \textbf{Whether to accept the $[4.0, 5.0)$ conditional verdict} or
  narrow the claimed domain further (\S\ref{sec:verdict},
  \S\ref{sec:phisupervision}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The three ideas to carry, if you carry nothing else}
\label{sec:threeideas}

\textbf{One.} \emph{Silicon burning is a rearrangement, and everything
algebraic in this project is a consequence.} \nuc{Si}{28} does not fuse
appreciably with \nuc{Si}{28} at any temperature the star survives, so the
flow proceeds by disassembly and reassembly through a light-particle
reservoir. Fast near-balanced pairs plus slow bridges \emph{is} $\kappa$,
\emph{is} stiffness, \emph{is} QSE, \emph{is} catastrophic cancellation ---
four names for one mechanism.

\textbf{Two.} \emph{\Ye{} is the whole point, and the strong sector cannot
change it.} The left null space of the strong/EM columns is exactly
$\mathrm{span}\{A, Z\}$, so every bit of \Ye{} evolution runs through 46/607
or 173/1518 weak columns, of which twenty carry essentially all of it. That is
why the weak sector is never maskable, why conservation must be structural
rather than penalised, and why the gates are absolute rather than relative.

\textbf{Three.} \emph{The training distribution and the deployment
distribution are different worlds, and it is measured.} $\kappa \approx 1$
everywhere on the Sobol grid; a $\kappa$-continuum from $10^{-3}$ to
$10^{-1}$ on the relaxed manifold. Relaxation \emph{onto} the slow manifold
and motion \emph{along} it are different functions, not a covariate shift. No
architecture fixes that; only data from the right distribution does.

\subsection{Where Tier 3 hands off}
\label{sec:handsoff}

Tier~0 gave the substrate, Tier~1 the microphysics, Tier~2 the algebra,
Tier~3 the astrophysics and the verdict. What remains is \textbf{Tier~4 ---
the emulator}, which has no study node at all (\tierone{VIII.E.1} sketches
S17--S22).

It inherits a decided target, a conservation map that provably costs nothing,
a loss-weighting map, two architecture components whose stated premises have
been retired by measurement, an unmeasured accumulation slope that moves the
operative gate by $100\times$, an undeclared validity domain, and a training
distribution that is measurably not the deployment distribution.

That is a good position to be in. Every one of those is \emph{known}, and
every one was found before a single model was trained.
