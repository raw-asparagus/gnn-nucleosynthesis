``conservation is by construction'' &
conserved quantities \textbf{are} the left null space of $\nu$;
left-nullity$(\nu) = 1 = \mathrm{span}\{A\}$, left-nullity$(\nu$ restricted to
strong$) = 2 = \mathrm{span}\{A, Z\}$, left-nullity$(\nut) = 3 =
\mathrm{rowspace}(C)$ exactly --- so $C$ is \textbf{complete}
(\S\ref{sec:rank}) \\

``the tests demand \code{1e-12}'' &
column sums are integer arithmetic in float64 and are exactly 0.0; only the
random-\netflux{} sweep needs a tolerance, and its two-regime form (absolute at
$O(1)$, relative-to-gross at \code{1e-20}) is what makes it non-vacuous
(\S\ref{sec:exactzero}) \\

``Target B projects onto the constraint manifold'' &
$P$ is the \emph{minimum-Euclidean-correction} projector, derived from a
Lagrangian; the QR form avoids squaring $\cond(C) \approx 200$
(\S\ref{sec:projector}) \\

``Target A and Target B are different designs'' &
\textbf{$\image(\nut) = \operatorname{null}(C)$} --- same reachable set. The
trade is parameterisation (528-dim fibre), conditioning
($\cond\nut \approx 41/55$), and physical structure --- not expressivity
(\S\ref{sec:equivalence}) \\

``\kap{} is the cancellation ratio'' &
$\kap = |\tanh(\aff/2kT)|$ --- a monotone reparameterisation of the reaction
affinity in units of $kT$; hence $\kap \approx \varepsilon/2$ for a relative
rate error $\varepsilon$, $1/\kap$ as the error-amplification factor, and the
$\tanh$ form of the screening offset (\S\ref{sec:kappa}) \\

``$\nu R$ sums over reactions'' &
$\nu R = \sum_{\rm forward \cup unpaired} \nu\netflux$ exactly, because
$\nu_{\cdot \mathrm{pair}(j)} = -\nu_{\cdot j}$; this is what licenses
restricting every coverage statistic to net columns
(\S\ref{sec:netidentity}) \\

``$\kap \approx 1$ on the training grid'' &
\ldots because Sobol compositions are nucleon-loaded, affinities are $\gg kT$,
and \kap{} saturates. The pass is \textbf{vacuous}, the cancellation structure
lives on relaxed states only, and the $\dd t = 10^{-6}$ grid handshake premise
is void (\S\ref{sec:vacuous}) \\

``NSE is Saha'' &
$\log C_i$ derived term-by-term from the grand-canonical distribution, with
$B_i$ emerging from rest-mass bookkeeping; nuclei are classical
($n\lambda^3 \approx 3\times10^{-7}$) while electrons are degenerate
(\S\ref{sec:ensemble}--\S\ref{sec:saha}) \\

``the solver falls back to bisection'' &
$\dd\Ye/\dd\up|_{\Sigma X=1} = W^2 \Var_w(Z/A)/(kT S_N) > 0$ --- a proof, which
also explains the 2\,MeV damping cap and identifies the degenerate case
(\S\ref{sec:monotonicity}) \\

``QSE adds a cluster offset'' &
\uG{} is the log over-population of the group relative to NSE; median
$+4.3$\,MeV $\Rightarrow$ ${\sim}10^{5}\times$ over-populated mid-burn;
$\uG = 0$ recovers NSE and that is a test (\S\ref{sec:qsecluster}) \\

``QSE reduces the network'' &
QSE \textbf{is} the slow manifold of a singular perturbation; stiffness ratio
$= 1/\epsilon$; the group mass is the slow coordinate; hence ``stiffness and QSE
are the same phenomenon'' (\S\ref{sec:slowmanifold}) \\

``\Phifl{} labels come from the integrator'' &
\ldots because they \emph{have} to: $\nu\Phifl = \Delta Y$ has a 528- (1368-)
dimensional solution set, so \Phifl{} is not recoverable from the shipped labels
at all (\S\ref{sec:phinotrecoverable}) \\

``the energy check validates the flux route'' &
the two routes collapse algebraically given $\Delta Y = \nu\Phifl$; the residual
measures \textbf{nuclear-data self-consistency}, not dynamics
(\S\ref{sec:energyidentity}) --- and \S\ref{sec:wegscheider} measures that
inconsistency: rms 0.55\,keV, max 3.5\,keV, 331/561 columns \\

``the rate set is detailed-balance consistent'' &
\textbf{pairwise yes, globally no.} $Q \notin \mathrm{rowspace}(\nu)$ for the
strong sector, so no mass table reproduces the tabulated $Q$'s; this is a
Wegscheider violation and it predicts a \kap{} data floor of
$6\times10^{-4}\ldots4\times10^{-3}$ that brackets the measured stock-MESA
\kap{} floor (\S\ref{sec:wegscheider}) \\

``$\kap > 0.1$ selects the reactions that matter'' &
\kap{} alone is flux-blind. $\sigma_j = 2\kB s_j \kap_j \artanh \kap_j$ is the
entropy production, quadratic in \kap{} and \textbf{linear in the gross flux}
--- an entropy-weighted active set is parameter-free and cannot admit a
high-\kap, no-flux column. It is defined on \textbf{strong\,/\,EM pairs only}:
weak columns have $\kap \equiv 1$ by construction, so $\sigma$ diverges there
and they must be admitted structurally, exactly as they are today
(\S\ref{sec:kappa-entropy}, \S\ref{sec:entropy}, \S\ref{sec:weakkappa}) \\

``the composition is 80- or 151-dimensional'' &
the \emph{visited} manifold has effective dimension $\approx 3.4$\,/\,8.6
(participation ratio); 7\,/\,14 PCs carry 90\% of $\Var(X)$. But the strong
sector is \Ye-neutral by \S\ref{sec:rank}, so \textbf{\Ye{} hides in a long
low-variance tail}: by $k = 30$, $\Var(X)$ is at 99.7\%/98.1\% and $\Var(\Ye)$
stalls at 93.0\%/95.3\%, against a budget needing $1 - 6.5\times10^{-8}$ ---
variance-truncated compression is arithmetically excluded
(\S\ref{sec:effdim}) \\

``657k samples'' &
$\approx 300$ independent physical systems. Per-row quantities have ICC
0.22--0.70 across trajectories (design effect 6.5--18.6), so every p-value and
CI in the kill-test block is unstated or wrong --- point estimates and the
empty-mask result survive (\S\ref{sec:clusterrobust}, \reg{R-08}) \\

``the regime box is derived edge by edge'' &
its \emph{support} is; its \textbf{measure} is not. The 20\,\Msun{} track sits
within 0.12--0.18\,dex of a \emph{line}, and only 35\%\,/\,62\%\,/\,82\% of the
box lies within 0.1\,/\,0.2\,/\,0.3\,dex of a visited point --- the training
density is uniform where the physical density is a band
(\S\ref{sec:samplingmeasure}, \reg{R-07}) \\

``conservation-by-construction makes the update physical'' &
it makes it \emph{conserving}. It does not make it \textbf{positive}
(\S\ref{sec:positivity}) and it does not make it obey the \textbf{second law}
--- a learned $\hat\netflux$ can point up the affinity gradient and pass every
gate in \code{CLAUDE.md} (\S\ref{sec:secondlaw}). The second-law guard is
available on strong\,/\,EM pairs; on the weak sector there is no
detailed-balance affinity to guard with, so the blind spot is total exactly
where \Ye{} lives (\S\ref{sec:secondlaw}) \\

\subsection*{The three ideas to carry, if you carry nothing else}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{The three ideas to carry}
\label{sec:threeideas}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Conservation is a rank statement.} Conserved quantities are the
  left null space of $\nu$; the measurement says that space is exactly
  3-dimensional in the extended system and exactly equals $\mathrm{rowspace}(C)$.
  Nothing is missing, nothing is redundant, and the whole thing is integer
  arithmetic --- which is why the tests can assert \code{== 0.0} and why no loss
  term is involved.
\item \textbf{$\kap = |\tanh(\aff/2kT)|$.} One identity that makes \kap{}
  simultaneously a thermodynamic distance from equilibrium, a detailed-balance
  error gauge ($\kap \approx \varepsilon/2$), and a condition number
  (amplification $1/\kap$). If you can write it down cold,
  \S\ref{part:fluxes} reconstructs itself --- and so does the screening offset,
  the v-flag floor, and the \kap$\leftrightarrow\delta$ correlation.
\item \textbf{Stiffness, QSE, and cancellation are one phenomenon.} A stiff
  system has an attracting low-dimensional manifold; the manifold is the
  equilibrium; the fast directions off it are the near-balanced pairs whose
  gross fluxes cancel. Every difficulty in this project --- the integrator's
  cost, the emulator's conditioning, the kill-test's premise, the training-grid
  distribution shift --- is one face of that single structure.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{And one habit, from the negative results}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{And one habit, from the negative results}
\label{sec:habit-negative}

Tier~1's closing habit was \emph{ask of any green test what would still be wrong
if it passed.} Tier~2's is its mirror, and this tier produced three occasions to
use it:

\begin{invariant}
\textbf{Ask of any negative result whether the instrument could have produced it
anyway.}
\end{invariant}

The vacuous \kap{} pass (\S\ref{sec:vacuous}) --- the instrument was fine, the
\emph{distribution} was wrong. The empty maskable set (\S\ref{sec:negresults})
--- the instrument was fine, the \emph{reference} was displaced. The absent
\rQSE{} plateau (\S\ref{sec:departure}, \reg{R-18}) --- here I genuinely do not
know, and the honest move was to write down the null distribution I would need
and log it rather than accept the verdict. A negative result is a measurement of
the instrument and the world jointly; the tier that teaches that is worth its
length.

\subsection*{And one more, from building the register}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{And one more, from building the register}
\label{sec:habit-register}

\S\ref{part:register} exists because of an exercise that is distinct from
studying, and does not happen unless it is scheduled:

\begin{invariant}
\textbf{Ask what the \emph{problem} needs, not what the plan lists.}
\end{invariant}

A study map is a plan for covering known territory. It is silent by construction
about what is not on it, and the silence looks exactly like coverage. Ripping
the map out and asking what the problem needs found twenty-five open items, five
of them measurable in an afternoon on data already on disk.

\S\ref{sec:auditmethod} adds the refinement that makes it repeatable: \textbf{a
single audit sweeps one axis}, so enumerate axes first and sweep each. And the
stopping rule is not ``I cannot think of more'' --- it is the ratio in
\S\ref{sec:converging}: \textbf{track how many candidate axes come back already
covered.} On the first pass almost none did; on the second, thirteen of
twenty-two. That number, not the item count, is what says the audit is
converging.
