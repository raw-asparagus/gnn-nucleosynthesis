\section*{Threads that carry forward}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Threads that carry forward}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{From the substrate and the network object
      (Parts \ref{part:substrate}--\ref{part:network}):}
      $\Gamma \approx 0.4$--25 fixes the screening prescription (\textbf{S4});
      partition functions at $kT \approx 0.3$~MeV fix the pf gate
      (\textbf{S3}); chemical equilibrium $\sum\nu_i\mu_i = 0$ generates NSE and
      QSE (\textbf{S9}); $\Delta N$ per REACLIB chapter is the index of the
      gh-575 bug (\textbf{S3}); the $\alpha$-ladder and two-cluster topology is
      what the group/bridge machinery measures (\textbf{S9}, \textbf{S13}); and
      the timescale hierarchy --- \emph{with} its measured absence of a clean
      spectral gap --- is the premise every reduction scheme must argue against
      (\textbf{S9}, \textbf{S14}).

\item \textbf{From the species algebra (\textbf{S0},
      Part~\ref{part:network}):} the linearity of $\sum A_i Y_i = 1$ in $Y$
      makes the conservation layer a fixed matrix --- \textbf{S7} turns that
      into $\nu$, $C$, and $P$.

\item \textbf{From the dataset (\textbf{S1}, Part~\ref{part:dataset}):}
      ``$\dd t = 10^{-6}$~s'' is already a full stiff relaxation, which forces
      the time-integrated flux target $\Phi = \int\varphi\,\dd t$ ---
      \textbf{S8} and \textbf{S10}.

\item \textbf{From the frame (Part~\ref{part:frame}):} $\kappa$ is a condition
      number (\textbf{S8}, \textbf{S14}); stiffness, QSE, and the reachable
      manifold are one structure (\textbf{S9}, \textbf{S10}, \textbf{S14}).
\end{itemize}

\section*{The three ideas to carry, if you carry nothing else}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{The three ideas to carry}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{$\kappa$ is a condition number} (\S\ref{sec:cancellation}), and
      independently the numerical test of detailed balance
      (\S\ref{sec:detailedbalance}). Every $\kappa$ threshold is a statement
      about tolerable error amplification; the kill-test is a conditioning
      study.
\item \textbf{Stiffness $\iff$ slow manifold $\iff$ QSE $\iff$ the reachable
      set} (\S\ref{sec:stiffness}, \S\ref{sec:onezone}). Four vocabularies, one
      object.
\item \textbf{The training distribution is not the deployment distribution}
      (\S\ref{sec:onezone}). The Sobol box covers composition space; physics
      lives on a low-dimensional manifold inside it. Several project findings
      are corollaries.
\end{enumerate}
