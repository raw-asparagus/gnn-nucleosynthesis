\part{Working practice: the discipline the repo runs on}
\label{part:practice}

\textbf{The one non-physics part.} It is here because you cannot read this
codebase without it: several modules look like over-engineering until you know
which epistemic rule they enforce. Every pattern below appears in code you will
read in Tier 1.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
\chapter{Every number carries a provenance label}
\label{sec:provenance}

Exactly one of:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l R{0.875} R{1.125}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Label} & \textbf{Meaning} &
\textbf{Failure mode it prevents} \\
\midrule
\textbf{sourced} & has a citation --- paper plus where in it &
  laundering an estimate into a citation's vicinity so it reads as
  authoritative \\
\textbf{derived} & names the script in \code{scripts/} that produces it &
  a number nobody can regenerate \\
\textbf{measured} &
  measured in this project, with a \code{RESULTS.md} row carrying date, commit,
  and data provenance &
  a number that drifts as the code changes \\
\textbf{assumed} &
  an internal estimate, stated \textbf{with its retirement plan} &
  an assumption hardening into a fact by repetition \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

The \code{assumed} category is the interesting one, and \S\ref{sec:timescales}
gave you its canonical example: the ``6--8 orders of magnitude'' timescale
separation was carried as assumed, with a checklist row naming the measurement
that would retire it --- and then that measurement retired it, in the opposite
direction from the assumption. \textbf{That is the system working}, and it only
worked because the number was never allowed to be quoted as sourced.

\chapter{Thresholds must have instruments}
\label{sec:instruments}

Any gate written down --- per-step $|\dYe| \lesssim 3\times10^{-6}$,
$\cond(S_{\mathrm{active}}) > 10^{6}$, mask churn $> 5\%$/step, the $2\times$
transfer falsifier --- must name the test, script, or checklist item that
measures it.

\begin{invariant}
A threshold with no measuring instrument is not a standard, it is a slogan.
\end{invariant}

This is why the Phase-0 checklist is a \emph{measurement program} rather than a
task list: each row is a quantity, the gate it sets, and the script that will
produce it. When you see a number in \code{CLAUDE.md}, look for its instrument;
if you cannot find one, that is a finding.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
\chapter{Decisions carry their own reversal condition}
\label{sec:switchconditions}

Settled decisions become ADRs, and every ADR must state a \textbf{switch
condition}: the measured threshold at which the decision reverses.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}R{1.000} l@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Statement} & \textbf{Verdict} \\
\midrule
``We chose Target A'' & \emph{incomplete} \\
``We chose Target A; we abandon it when
  $\cond(S_{\mathrm{active}}) > 10^{6}$ or $>90\%$ of net-flux-carrying
  reactions fall below the \Ye{} floor'' & \emph{complete} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

Accepted ADRs are immutable; a changed decision \emph{supersedes} rather than
rewrites, so the history of what was believed when survives. The same reasoning
governs measurements: a redone measurement never silently overwrites the old
value.

\chapter{Invariants live in code that refuses, not in comments}
\label{sec:guards}

The pattern you will meet repeatedly: rather than documenting a rule and hoping,
make the violation \textbf{inexpressible}.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}R{0.8} R{1.2}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Rule} & \textbf{How it is enforced} \\
\midrule
No raw pf-free v-flag reverses at $\Tn \geq 3$ &
  \code{fluxes/guards.py::assert\_pf\_gate} raises \code{PfGateError} at compile
  time \\
Never join labels on (\code{logT}, \code{logRho}) &
  \code{data/labels.py::join\_on\_state\_id} refuses non-\code{state\_id}-indexed
  frames \\
Weak columns are never maskable &
  \code{qse/diagnostics.py::eligible\_mask} excludes them \emph{structurally},
  from \code{weak\_mask}, so \code{killtest.active\_set.guidry\_masks} cannot
  produce one \\
Never mix the two $\kappa$ conventions &
  \code{killtest/distributions.py} refuses runs recording a screening config
  unless explicitly overridden \\
Conservation must hold for any $\varphi$ &
  \code{tests/test\_conservation.py} is a \textbf{blocking gate} run against a
  \emph{randomly initialized} flux head \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

Note the difference between a \emph{test} and a \emph{gate}. A test reports; a
gate blocks. The conservation check is a gate: training does not begin until it
passes, and edits to \code{graph/} trigger it automatically.

\chapter{Non-regenerable artifacts are persisted, not re-derived}
\label{sec:persistence}

\S\ref{sec:sobol} established that the Sobol grid cannot be regenerated. The
general rule that follows:

\begin{invariant}
When an artifact cannot be reproduced from a recipe, \textbf{persist the
artifact}, not the recipe.
\end{invariant}

Hence \code{configs/step5\_subsample\_*\_ids.json} holds explicit
\code{state\_id} lists even though \code{stratified\_sample} takes a seed ---
because seeded reproducibility depends on library versions, while a list of
integers does not. Same reasoning behind content hashes recorded for every
generated flux store.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
\chapter{Notebooks explore; scripts produce}
\label{sec:notebooks}

Notebooks are \textbf{exploratory only}. Every citable number comes from
\code{scripts/} and lands in \code{RESULTS.md}; notebooks load cached artifacts
and re-plot them.

This is enforced structurally too --- \code{nbsupport.caption()} raises if a
figure cites no \code{RESULTS.md} row, and the $\kappa$ helper requires a
declared UNSCREENED/SCREENED convention that must match the store's own
attributes, so mixing conventions fails loudly rather than silently.

\chapter*{Self-check --- working practice}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Self-check --- working practice}
\label{sec:selfcheck-practice}

\begin{selfcheck}
\item Pick five numbers from \code{CLAUDE.md} and classify each as sourced /
      derived / measured / assumed. For any \code{assumed}, find its retirement
      plan.
\item Take the $\cond(S_{\mathrm{active}}) > 10^{6}$ gate and name its
      instrument, its ADR, and its switch condition.
\item Find one guard in \code{fluxes/guards.py} and write the failure it makes
      impossible. Then write the comment-only version and explain what would
      eventually go wrong with it.
\item Why does \code{NSEResult} record \code{method} (\code{"newton"} /
      \code{"bisection"})? Connect to \S\ref{sec:newton} and to
      \S\ref{sec:instruments}.
\item Argue the cost side honestly: what does this discipline make \emph{slower}
      or harder, and when would it be the wrong trade?
\end{selfcheck}
