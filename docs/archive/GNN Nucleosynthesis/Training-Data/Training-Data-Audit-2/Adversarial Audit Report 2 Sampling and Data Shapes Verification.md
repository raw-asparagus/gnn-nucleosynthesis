## 3. THREE SEPARATED LISTS

### (a) MUST-FIX ERRORS
1. **"QSE holds for T ≳ 3 GK" misattributed to Hix & Thielemann 1996.** Belongs to Hix & Thielemann **1999** ("Silicon Burning II," ApJ 511:862). The Architecture Spec is right here; Report 2 is wrong.
2. **Fe-peak range "A≈45–65" — not in the source.** True: ≈A=45/50 up to top of network (Ge, ~A=78).
3. **QSE-window "3.3–5 GK" — fabricated/unsourced.** "3.3" appears nowhere in Hix & Thielemann 1996; "3.5–5 GK" is only that study's constant-T cube.
4. **Storage band "0.4–0.8 TB for both / 0.15–0.4 TB per network" — internally inconsistent.** Correct figure ≈ 0.14–0.22 TB; mesa_80 alone is 0.048–0.072 TB.
5. **Per-step-gate "~2 orders of magnitude" pairing mixes N=1.6×10³ and N=10⁴.** Restate at one N (√N scaling).
6. **Finding 2 "the whole three-way mixture is exactly EMCS" — overstatement** contradicting the report's own ~25%-component treatment.
7. **Guidry citation year "2012" → 2011** (arXiv:1112.4778); confirm the cancellation passage is in that synthesis paper vs the partial-equilibrium paper 1112.4738.
8. **"Dubins' theorem" likely mis-invoked** for a dimension argument that is elementary.
9. **Chemulator stabilizer mislabeled** as "Gaussian noise injection (+PCA)"; the paper says a training-time **"noise layer"** and an autoencoder alone (33→8).

### (b) FEASIBILITY RISKS / STATED-AS-FACT-BUT-NEEDS-LOCAL-CONFIRMATION
- **Variable-dt "nearly free / multiplier ≈1.0–1.3 / no new burns" (TL;DR, Finding 5, §D) — the single most dangerous error.** The marginal claim (one burn checkpointed 24× ≈ checkpointed 9×) is plausibly true, but the public Zenodo set stored **only 9 checkpoints**; dense intermediate states were not saved. Getting 16–24 checkpoints for the existing ~10⁶ Sobol states needs **either** the on-request 4 TB superset (discretionary, sufficiency unconfirmed) **or** re-running all ~10⁶ burns — a fresh campaign Report 1 costs at "few × 10⁵ CPU-hr" (6–29 node-months). This **conflates marginal per-burn cost with the project's actual cost** and **contradicts Report 1's "trajectory sequences = needs-new-bbq-runs, cluster-scale" tag. Report 1 is right.**
- **Whether bbq can cheaply emit dense intermediate checkpoints from one stiff integration**, and **whether a single burn to 10² s resolves the early stiff stage (to 10^-6 s) finely enough to checkpoint accurately** — LOCAL bbq-confirmation items (Report 1 flags bbq output columns/inlist controls as unverified), not literature facts.
- **4 TB superset density/timestep sufficiency** — discretionary external dependency, not a deposited fact; does not advertise fluxes or equilibrium abundances.
- **EMCS temperature-gradient thresholds** — confirmed one-shot/cheap, but the DeePODE-nuclear filter bands are tuned to white-dwarf combustion and need local re-tuning for the QSE regime.
- **Transfer of DeePODE-nuclear's "≲1%" to this project** — it is **composition** accuracy on an **alpha network with no weak reactions and no Ye**, so it does NOT exercise the Ye-controlling weak physics that is the project's entire point. Report 2 should flag this explicitly, not merely note "thresholds need re-tuning."
- **Rollout-fix transfer** — the EXISTENCE of rollout drift is settled cross-domain (Chemulator, MACE, van de Bor, MP-PDE), but whether the specific FIXES (noise layer + trained iterative update + pushforward) work for stiff QSE with weak-reaction Ye is a LOCAL experiment, not literature-settled.
- **Unverified-this-pass:** SkyNet/WinNet method descriptions; ECSN/PPISN/PISN regime numbers; the nuclear paper's λ=0.1-down-to-O(10^-25), 17M/28M datapoints, and gradient-retention percentages; NuGNN signed-log/conservation details. Student to confirm against source PDFs.
